Warna kuning/amber padat (`#f59e0b` / `#fbbf24` / `#f5a623`).
- **UI & Design Invariant — Zero AI-Slop (Flat Minimalist Gen-Z & Millennial)**:
  - **Decorative badge icons & Sparkles are AI slop**: NEVER use `Sparkles`, `lucide-sparkles`, magic stars, wands, or decorative mini badge icons inside kickers, buttons, filter dropdowns, toolbar controls, or section headers. They look like cheap generative AI slop. Use pure, crisp typography or authentic functional domain icons only (e.g. `Droplets` for hydration/formula, `Layers` for collections, `ArrowUpDown` for sorting, `Shield` for warranty/safety, `Truck` for shipping, `Box` for packaging).
  - **No bubbly rounded-3xl / rounded-2xl or pastel gradient containers**: Avoid bloated bubbly border-radii (`rounded-3xl`), bubble pill badges (`rounded-full bg-...`), and pastel gradients (`bg-gradient-to-br`). Modern high-end Gen-Z & Millennial aesthetic (Rhode, Glossier, Byredo, Aesop, SSENSE) is flat, sharp or micro-radius (`rounded-none` to `rounded-sm`), architectural whitespace, and high-contrast typography.
  - **No skeuomorphic gimmicks (pushpins, sticky notes, tilted cards, fake meter bars)**: NEVER simulate physical objects (such as 3D pushpins, taped paper, rotated/tilted memo cards, corkboards) or invent arbitrary percentage progress bars (e.g. "35% Recovery Barrier", "70% Surface Refinement"). Timelines, milestone journeys, and steps must be displayed as clean, architectural linear progressions (minimal grid or vertical index `01 / 02 / 03`, crisp borders, solid contrast, genuine factual copy).
- **UI & Design Invariant — No Divider Lines Between Sections**: NEVER add horizontal divider lines, divider borders, or strokes (`border-t`, `border-b`, `<hr>`) between sections or directly above/below hero and footer sections. Section transitions must remain natural, clean, and seamless, relying strictly on whitespace, spacing, and subtle background color dissolves, never harsh horizontal dividing borders.
- **UI & Design Invariant — No Contact-CTA Spam (WhatsApp)**: At most ONE WhatsApp/contact call to action per page, placed after the user's task is done (tool result, end of article/product, form submit). NEVER in header, menu, hero, sticky bars, floating buttons, pop-ups, or repeated per list item. Footer may list the number as plain contact text. Use a secondary-style button with a monochrome icon, not brand-green WhatsApp styling. Sharing uses the native share sheet, not a second WhatsApp icon.
- **UI & Design Invariant — Article Metadata & Tags (Clean Editorial, Anti-Overspace, No Heavy Chips)**:
  - **Metadata, Not Buttons or Cards**: Article tags are taxonomical metadata, NEVER primary action buttons, status pills, or framed cards. Do NOT wrap individual tags in heavy container frames, bordered boxes, or filled card chips (e.g. `bg-tint`, borders, rounded pill badges).
  - **Clean Inline Typographic Flow**: Tags must render as a clean, compact inline typographic list: prefixed with a crisp, uppercase label (`Tag:` in muted or soil accent color), followed by natural text links with a subtle `#` hashtag prefix. Interactivity uses subtle color transition to brand color and text underline (`hover:underline underline-offset-4`), keeping the reading experience calm and editorial.
  - **Strict Anti-Overspace (No Double Margins & No Inflated min-height)**:
    - NEVER apply isolated touch-target buttons `min-h-[44px]` to inline tag links. Forcing 44px min-height on multi-tag wraps inflates the tag block vertically to 120px–150px+ of dead space on mobile screens. Use compact line-height with minimal padding (`py-0.5` to `py-1`).
    - Account for the natural `margin-bottom` of prose closing paragraphs (`p:last-child`). Never stack large top margins (`mt-8`, `mt-10`) on top of prose bottom margins; keep the top spacing tight (`mt-3` to `mt-4`) and bottom rhythm balanced so the tags feel like a natural paragraph footer rather than an abandoned floating element.


- **SEO Invariant — Title & Meta Description Length (all websites, owner rule 2026-09-30)**: final rendered `<title>` is **55–70 characters including spaces and the brand suffix** (`{Page title} - {Brand}`; home may omit the suffix); meta description is **120–155 characters including spaces**, ends with a full stop. Separator is ` - ` (or `:` inside a title), **never `|` or `—`**. Pad a short title/description with related, natural keywords (topic, commodity, place, intent), never with hype words ("Rahasia", "Ampuh", "Tuntas", "100%") or keyword lists. Generated titles (tags, hubs, pagination) use a fit-to-range helper, and the build check fails outside the ranges. Every page also carries `<meta name="author">` (article author, otherwise the brand/publisher name) and `<meta name="publisher">`; articles add `article:author` and `article:publisher`, and JSON-LD links `author` → Person and `publisher` → Organization with a raster logo. Reference implementation: agritani `src/lib/seo.ts` `fitText()` + `scripts/check-seo.mjs` (DEC-022, T-45).
- **SEO Invariant — Images: WebP + Keyword ALT (all websites, owner rule 2026-10-01)**: every page image is **WebP** (SVG for icons; only platform-required PNGs such as OG images and `apple-touch-icon.png` stay PNG). Every content `<img>` has a descriptive ALT of **10–125 characters** containing the page's keyword (commodity/topic/product + what the image shows), never empty, a lone generic word ("gambar", "foto", "ilustrasi"), or a keyword list. Dynamic pages build ALT from their data (e.g. `Ilustrasi {commodity}: {title}`, `Kemasan {product}, {role}`). A build check fails on a non-WebP source or a bad ALT. Reference implementation: agritani `src/lib/image-alt.ts` + `scripts/check-images.mjs` (T-54).
- **UI & Design Invariant — Icons**: use inline SVG copied from the Lucide set (one stroke style, `aria-hidden`, `currentColor`), not an icon React/JS package, on static or non-React sites; functional icons only (no decorative sparkles/badges). In an existing React app, `lucide-react` is acceptable when already installed.
