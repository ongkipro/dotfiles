## Lesson: Shopee CPAS purchase columns, `7d_click` vs `1d_view`, Pure Carousel vs Collection (`da_collection`), and in-place creative hot-swap

- **Symptom & root cause:** On Shopee CPAS accounts, ordinary `actions` looked empty while orders lived in `catalog_segment_actions` / `catalog_segment_value` (`1d_view` heavy), Instagram WebView logged `app_site_visit` without native app arrival, Ads Manager hid `Website and app` (Error 100 / Subcode 3858845) without `is_pca_unified=true` + `promoted_object`, and `facebook_reels` opened `da_collection` grids when `template_url_spec` or `asset_feed_spec` was missing ([cpas-collaborative-ads.md](../../../skills/local/meta-ads-cli/references/cpas-collaborative-ads.md) §7).
- **Rule & fix:** Query CPAS with `--metric-profile cpas_v1`, read `catalog_segment_actions:omni_purchase` (`7d_click` beside total), and verify native app arrival via `app_custom_event.fb_mobile_content_view / link_click`. `dotads-meta deploy` creates CPAS campaigns via the Python SDK with `is_pca_unified=true`, `smart_promotion_type='GUIDED_CREATION'`, and `promoted_object`, AdSets with `ad_set_goal={'type': 0}` + `omnichannel_object`, and catalog creatives via `AdAccount.create_ad_creative` with `template_url_spec.config.app_id`, `asset_feed_spec: {"ad_formats": ["CAROUSEL"], "optimization_type": "FORMAT_AUTOMATION"}`, `format_transformation_spec` (`carousel` + `catalog_single_media` without `da_collection`), and `contextual_multi_ads: {"enroll_status": "OPT_IN"}`. **DO:** Use Pure Carousel (`dotads: Carousel | ...`) for 1-click Shopee PDP cards; isolate Collection (`dotads: Collection | ...`, `COLLECTION_VIDEO`) in its own `1-1-1` campaign with a dedicated 9:16 hero video + Instant Experience Canvas. **DON'T:** Mix Carousel + auto `da_collection`, or recreate Campaign/Ad Set just to fix a creative — hot-swap `Ad(ad_id).api_update(params={"creative": {"creative_id": new_cr_id}})` in-place.

## Lesson: Adaptive Website Conversion (`OUTCOME_SALES`) Liquidity, Multi-Horizon Timing (`>= 24h`), Scaling & Signal Coupling

- **Principle (priors to calibrate per business model and unit economics, never a rigid lock):**
  1. **CLI Campaign Prefix (`dotads: `):** Every campaign created via `dotads-meta` (`deploy` or `run -- ads campaign create`) carries the `dotads: <campaign_name>` label (`validate` warns if omitted; `deploy`/`run` auto-prefixes `dotads: `).
  2. **Auction Liquidity vs. Hard Controls:** Over-filtering (`advantage_audience: 0`, narrow age/gender locks, unnecessary placement exclusions, or click-only attribution) shrinks Andromeda retrieval pools and raises clearing CPM/CPC. Use **0 interests + `advantage_audience: 1` + Broad + Advantage+ Placements + `OFFSITE_CONVERSIONS` + `7d_click + 1d_view (+ 1d_ev)`** as the starting baseline; apply hard `Audience Controls` only for verified legal/age floors, geographic serviceability/margin boundaries, >15% measured demographic leakage, or technical app-routing constraints.
  3. **Multi-Horizon Audit Timing (`>= 24h` Diurnal Floor):** Intraday pacing exploration and conversion lag make `<24h` performance pauses unreliable. Use `0–6h` for technical sanity checks only; require `>= 24h` (`>= 1` full diurnal cycle) + declared test spend (`>= 1.0x Target_CPA` or owner stop-loss floor) before proposing a zero-intent stop-loss (`0 Conversions` and `ATC <= 1`); hold ad sets with active mid-funnel intent (`ATC` / `IC`) to `48–72h`; and judge scaling or retirement on `3–7d` (`last_7d`) cohorts.
  4. **Coupled Scaling & Funnel Velocity:** Scale Up vertically in `+15%–25%` steps every `48–72h` (transitioning from `LOWEST_COST_WITHOUT_CAP` to `COST_CAP` / `BID_CAP` / `MIN_ROAS` at higher budgets), and Scale Out horizontally via orthogonal creative angles (`3–5` concepts per ad set; avoid crowding `>6` ads on micro-budgets) and low-friction checkout flows (`LPV -> ATC -> IC -> Purchase`) paired with high-EMQ Pixel + CAPI (`event_id` 48h dedup). See [algorithm-and-learning.md](../../../skills/local/meta-ads-cli/references/algorithm-and-learning.md) §6–§7 and [PIXEL_2026_CHANGES.md](../../../skills/local/meta-ads-signal-engine/references/PIXEL_2026_CHANGES.md).

## Observation: CPAS × Shopee Affiliate is not necessarily omnichannel (2026-11-18 WIB)

- **Owner-requested learning; scope:** One authenticated, read-only campaign/ad-set/ad/creative inspection observed `is_pca_unified=true` with a catalog at campaign level, a PURCHASE pixel + product set at ad-set level **without a returned omnichannel_object**, and two manual single-video ads using Shopee affiliate short links and `applink_treatment=web_only`. Audience was broad Indonesia with Advantage+ audience, Facebook Feed/Reels only, and 7d click + 1d view attribution. Private IDs, budgets and raw evidence remain in the selected account workspace, not shared memory.
- **Qualification of the defaults below:** The omnichannel/deep-link blueprint remains a configuration-specific method, not a mandatory description of every CPAS × affiliate campaign. Inspect all four layers before planning; do not silently replace a pixel-only/web-only affiliate configuration with merchant omnichannel tracking. An Instagram identity on a creative does not itself mean Instagram placement delivery.
- **Limits:** ACTIVE status is a configuration observation, not proof of delivery, native-app arrival, promo validity, performance or profitability. Referenced catalog IDs can remain visible in campaign metadata even when the current API identity cannot read/manage those assets; do not infer that active references establish copy permissions. Divergent top-level and nested creative video IDs require rendered/media verification before identifying the actual video.
- **App-opening interpretation (owner clarification, not independently reproduced):** The reference Facebook Feed/Reels placements support opening Shopee's app. Do not infer "cannot open app" from `applink_treatment=web_only` alone: review the actual affiliate short-link redirect and mobile OS/app-link handoff separately from Meta's creative destination setting. Distinguish app-opening capability, observed app arrival and aggregate app-arrival rate; desktop/headless browsing cannot establish real mobile native-app behavior. Do not change placements or force omnichannel just from this field.
- **Measurement:** Validate event/value mapping for the actual setup and separate click/view attribution. Judge affiliate economics against valid/settled commissions minus ad spend, not merchant GMV alone. Empty ordinary purchase actions are insufficient to conclude zero Shopee outcomes. No winning structure or budget was established by this inspection.

- **Evidence owner:** Account-local `campaigns/gacorin-active-inspection.json` and `campaigns/gacorin-active-study.md`, linked from that account's `memory/MEMORY.md`. Re-read live configuration before reuse.

## Lesson: Shopee CPAS Hybrid Manual `1-1-2`, ONG Duplication, Tiered `RMK` Exclusions & Social-Proof Post Reuse

- **Symptom & root cause:** Cross-seller collaborative catalog rejected Shopee product URLs (`Error 100` invalid Shopee Business Account URL); app-only conversion location failed (`Subcode 1885010`); plain API ad set creation dropped `regional_regulated_categories: ["VOLUNTARY_VERIFICATION"]` and lookalike `0`; Meta silently reset `min_retention_seconds` inside `product_audience_specs.inclusions` to `"0"`; and reusing winning catalog posts via `object_story_id` (`Subcode 1990065`) or ad `/copies` (`Subcode 2490596`) failed ([cpas-collaborative-ads.md](../../../skills/local/meta-ads-cli/references/cpas-collaborative-ads.md) §7.6).
- **Rule & fix:** Keep omnichannel `Website and app` (`PURCHASE` count on Shopee App `957549474255294` + Pixel `466924370133774`, matching seller `product_set_id`). Duplicate a verified UI reference campaign + ad set via `/copies` (`deep_copy=false`, passing `start_time` inside the ad set copy call); attach manual single-video/image direct-product creatives with `omnichannel_link_spec`, `applink_treatment: "deeplink_with_web_fallback"`, `contextual_multi_ads: {"enroll_status": "OPT_OUT"}`, and `standard_enhancements` stripped. Size `RMK` from mature `"Interacted with Product Set"` `CLAIM` custom audiences (never `reachestimate` when `estimate_ready=false`), carve non-overlapping `0–7d / 7–14d / 14–30d` tiers via `excluded_custom_audiences` (`7d` / `14d`), exclude the `30d` warm custom audience on Cold ad sets, and reuse social-proof catalog posts via `creative: {"creative_id": "<original_creative_id>"}`.
