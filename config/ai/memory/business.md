# Business Memory
> Durable business context. Use this to avoid re-asking settled direction and to evaluate ideas against real strategic priorities.

## Business identity
- User operates at the intersection of ecommerce, software, AI, SEO, affiliate, and paid ads.
- Core pattern: turn services and workflows into reusable systems, assets, portals, platforms, prompts, SOPs, and AI-assisted operations.

## Main business directions

### 1. Indonesia Commerce / Commerce Portal OS
- Direction: platform/infrastructure for Indonesian ecommerce operators.
- Conceptual positioning: Shopify-like store infrastructure combined with WeTracked-like tracking/form/data infrastructure.
- Areas discussed: super admin, client admin, public frontend/store/blog, dynamic form, area detection, COD control, tracking, pixel, dashboard, client/admin workflow.
- Technical direction: Astro + Tailwind for public frontend when SEO/static speed matters; Next.js + shadcn/ui for admin/client dashboards when interaction complexity matters.

### 2. KIHeute
- Brand: KIHeute.
- Market: German-speaking audience.
- Positioning: practical AI for office and business productivity.
- Main audience: German office workers and German small business owners.
- Priorities: save time, save money, reduce manual work, improve productivity, understand practical AI tools, improve office/business workflows.
- Avoided topics: AI news, AI art, crypto, programming, gaming, student content, general AI trends, fake income claims, luxury lifestyle, aggressive sales content.
- Channel strategy discussed: YouTube faceless/Shorts, Pinterest, Medium, Facebook, TikTok Germany.
- Affiliate angle: AI/SaaS productivity tools and office/home-office products; examples discussed include Notion, Canva, Grammarly, Semrush, Monday, Amazon DE office products.

### 3. Germany affiliate engine
- Main market: Germany.
- Networks in scope: Digistore24, Impact, CJ, Awin, PartnerStack, Amazon Associates DE, ClickBank when suitable.
- Preferred traffic assets: SEO content, YouTube faceless, Pinterest, Medium, tool directory/review/comparison content.

### 4. Shopify/headless stores
- Active or repeated themes: Shopify standard stores, Shopify theme development, headless Shopify, Astro storefronts, product/category SEO, landing pages, conversion, tracking.
- Example project contexts discussed: Pixsgo/Play & Go, Petcue, Panna Coffee, Shopify service pitch deck, advertiser service pitch deck.

### 5. AI business content/asset creation
- Direction discussed: AI Marketing Asset Creator / AI Business Content Creator, especially service assets for local businesses or ecommerce brands.
- User tends to scale execution through interns and repeatable SOPs.

## Strategic decision filters
Before recommending a new business direction, evaluate:
1. Distribution: where traffic/users come from.
2. Monetization: affiliate, SaaS, service, marketplace, subscription, lead-gen, or hybrid.
3. Margin: manual workload vs automation leverage.
4. Compliance: platform policy, ad policy, affiliate rules, legal/financial risk.
5. Data/tracking: first-party tracking, pixel quality, attribution, customer ownership.
6. SEO moat: topical authority, internal linking, programmatic pages, indexing risk.
7. Execution: can user, interns, or AI agents operate it consistently?
8. Scalability: can it expand across niches, countries, or clients?

## Anti-patterns to challenge
- Broad portals with no distribution edge.
- Affiliate content without buyer intent.
- Fancy platform before validated demand.
- AI automation without clear SOP and data structure.
- Product import scale before SEO/category architecture and conversion logic.
- Paid ads without tracking/landing page/funnel discipline.
