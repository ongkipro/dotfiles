# Public Experience Visual Lens

Apply this optional visual review only after loading the canonical rules in [design-taste](../SKILL.md). This file owns no commerce behavior, application state, copy, SEO, or implementation contract.

## Ownership boundary

- `storefront-ux` owns discovery, filtering, variants, availability, cart, checkout, booking-like decision flows, recovery, and analytics behavior.
- `storefront-development` owns storefront implementation.
- `copywriting` and `content` own wording; `ai-traffic-os` owns AnswerBox and AEO/GEO rules.
- This reference only asks whether a public surface expresses the accepted behavior with deliberate hierarchy, imagery, typography, spacing, color, and motion.

## Storefront visual prompts

- Let product imagery and price/availability hierarchy lead; use borders, tints, or spacing before generic shadowed cards.
- Vary composition only when real merchandising content supports it. Do not invent promotional tiles or alter the list/filter behavior defined by `storefront-ux`.
- Make selected, unavailable, error, and pending states visually distinct while preserving their canonical semantics and accessible names.
- Keep the checkout action visually primary without implying that payment or order completion occurred.

## Travel and booking visual prompts

- Treat the search or booking control as the primary visual task, with legible fields, dates, totals, and focus states.
- Keep calendars and pricing breakdowns typographically clear; decorative treatment must not obscure availability, fees, or selection.
- Use imagery as context, not as a contrast-destroying backdrop for essential controls.

## Landing-page visual prompts

- Apply the mode, dials, hierarchy, theme, and anti-slop rules from `design-taste`; do not establish a second landing-page system here.
- Reserve motion for hierarchy, feedback, or storytelling and honor the canonical reduced-motion rules.
- Visual proof must use approved real assets and must not manufacture logos, reviews, metrics, or customer evidence.
