# Skincare and Beauty

Structure: [README.md](README.md). A starting hypothesis; inspected references
and the owner's brand overrule it.

## 1. Job and decision path

Visitors often arrive from social video, a creator review, or a marketplace
listing, already knowing a product name. Entry → "is this for my skin type and
concern?" → ingredients, texture, size, price per use, safety/registration →
buy (site, marketplace, or WhatsApp) → how to use, when to expect results,
reorder. Many first visits are comparison visits; the page must answer fast on a
phone.

## 2. Market conventions — VERIFY per project

- BPOM notification number shown per product (Indonesia), NPRA notification
  (Malaysia); halal certification where the brand holds it. Confirm the current
  display rules and the exact numbers from the owner's documents.
- Marketplace buttons beside on-site checkout are common; confirm which
  channel the owner wants to win.
- WhatsApp consultation ("which product fits my skin?") may be the real primary
  action for smaller brands.
- Bundles and routines (cleanser + serum + moisturizer) and shade or size
  variants; Rupiah or RM pricing with the per-ml or per-gram value if the owner
  wants comparison.
- Copy is often bilingual or mixes Bahasa with English ingredient names.

## 3. Must-have content inventory

- Real product photography: pack shot, texture/swatch, scale in hand; shade
  swatches on several real skin tones for colour products.
- Full ingredient list (INCI) and the key actives with their purpose.
- Skin type / concern fit, how to use, routine order, size, expiry or PAO.
- Registration and halal numbers, net content, manufacturer/distributor.
- Real reviews with their source; before/after images only with consent,
  unedited, and with the conditions stated.

## 5. Recurring patterns

| Problem | Pattern | Does not apply when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Is it for my skin?" decided in seconds | Concern/skin-type chips near the product name, linking to a filtered list | one hero product with a single use |
| Texture and colour cannot be felt online | Texture close-up, swatch row, short real-use video | supply is pack shots only (ask, do not generate) |
| Trust in a new local brand | Registration number, ingredient transparency, real reviews near price | the brand is already the trust anchor |
| Results questions | Before/after comparison slider with consented, unretouched images and stated time frame (motion-craft.md §5.1) | no consented images exist |
| Routine purchase | Bundle or routine builder with clear savings | single-SKU brand |

## 6. Category default to escape

Pastel or beige ground, a thin serif display face, floating product on a
blurred blob, "glow" and "clean" in every heading, generic three-icon benefit
row. Fine when the brand chose it; a default when it came from the category word.

## 7. Optional style directions

| Direction | Fit | Risk | A11y cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clinical clarity (white space, data-like ingredient tables) | actives-led, derm-adjacent brands | feels cold for lifestyle brands | low; watch light-grey text contrast |
| Editorial warmth (large photography, narrative) | lifestyle, founder-led brands | product facts buried under mood | text over photos needs measured contrast |
| Playful colour-blocking | youth, colour cosmetics | competes with shade accuracy | colour cannot be the only shade label |

## 8. Mobile notes

Price, variant, and buy action reachable without long scroll; swatches large
enough to tap; ingredient list in a disclosure, not a tiny table; marketplace
and WhatsApp buttons clearly labelled, not icon-only.

## 9. Claim traps

Medical or permanent-result claims ("cures acne", "permanently whitens"),
unqualified "100% natural", "dermatologist approved" without a named source,
percentages from unnamed studies, "No. 1" or "best seller" without data,
before/after images that are edited or not consented. Check local cosmetic
advertising rules with the owner.

## 10. Open questions

Primary sales channel? Registration and halal status per product? Which real
photography and reviews exist? Shade range and skin-tone imagery? Bilingual?
