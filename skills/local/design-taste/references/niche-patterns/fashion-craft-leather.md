# Fashion, Modest Apparel, Leather & Craft Goods

Covers: leather goods (bags, wallets, belts, footwear), modest fashion and
muslimwear (gamis, koko, hijab, sarimbit family sets), batik/tenun, and artisan
craft storefronts. Read [README.md](README.md) first.

## 1. Job and decision path

The buyer cannot touch the leather grain, feel fabric drape/breathability, or
try on the cut before delivery. The page must resolve **material authenticity**
and **size/capacity fit** before asking for payment or COD commitment:

1. **Inspect silhouette and true color** across selected variants.
2. **Verify material grade and construction** (leather cut/tanning or fabric
   weave/weight/lining, zipper/hardware quality, busui/wudhu functional details).
3. **Confirm fit or capacity** (local garment measurements in `cm` or bag/wallet
   compartment dimensions and what fits inside).
4. **Select variant(s)** (single item or multi-item family/sarimbit set) and
   complete purchase via cart, direct COD form, marketplace, or WhatsApp.

## 2. Market conventions — VERIFY per project

Confirm with the owner:

- **Garment measurement conventions:** local buyers expect centimeter tables
  using familiar abbreviations (`LD` Lingkar Dada, `PB` Panjang Baju, `PL`
  Panjang Lengan, `LK` Lingkar Ketiak, `LP` Lingkar Pinggang) plus reference body
  weight/height notes.
- **Modest-fashion functional attributes:** whether buyers filter or decide by
  `busui-friendly` (front zipper/button access), `wudhu-friendly` (cuff design),
  opacity (`tidak menerawang`), and lining (`furing`).
- **Coordinated family sets (Sarimbit / Couple):** whether items are sold as a
  fixed bundle or an independent per-member size/quantity matrix (father, mother,
  son, daughter) on a single PDP.
- **Leather & craft authenticity proof:** exact material grade (`full-grain`,
  `top-grain pull-up/crazy horse`, `split/genuine leather`, or `synthetic
  microfiber/PU`), hardware specs (`YKK metal zipper`, brass buckles), and
  stitching/warranty terms.
- **Exchange and COD policy:** size-exchange window (e.g. 3–7 days with unwashed
  tags) and whether COD is offered nationwide.

## 3. Must-have content inventory

- **True-to-color studio and worn/in-hand photography** for every active color
  variant.
- **Macro detail shots** showing leather grain, fabric weave, stitching, inner
  lining, and compartments/closures.
- **Exact material disclosure** and care instructions.
- **HTML size chart or dimension schematic** in centimeters (`P × L × T` for
  bags/wallets, `LD/PB/PL` for apparel, insole length in `cm` for footwear).
- **Scale reference:** model height/weight and worn size, or everyday objects
  that fit inside a bag/wallet (e.g. 14-inch laptop, unfolded banknotes, card
  slots).
- **Clear exchange, warranty, and shipping terms.**

Ask the owner for any missing item; never invent material grades, warranty
durations, or artisan origin stories.

## 5. Recurring patterns

| Problem | Pattern that solves it | When it does not apply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyers hesitate because "genuine leather" or "premium fabric" is overused by low-grade sellers | Name the exact material grade, tanning/weave type, hardware, and lining, paired with a macro texture/stitching photo | Novelty/fast-fashion items where price alone drives the impulse decision |
| High return/RTS rate from wrong size selection | Place the centimeter size guide trigger directly beside the size selector pills, with model height/weight reference and measurement instructions | One-size accessories (keychains, cardholders) — use physical `P × L × T` dimensions instead |
| Buyers cannot tell how much a bag or wallet actually holds | Interior cutaway/open-compartment photo plus a concise capacity list (laptop size, card slot count, bill compartment fit) | Apparel without functional pocket/storage claims |
| Family/sarimbit buyers abandon when forced to visit 4 separate product pages to buy matching outfits | Unified multi-variant matrix on one PDP allowing independent size and quantity selection per family role with a live running subtotal | Single-SKU standalone garments |
| Color names ("Havana", "Sage", "Terracotta") are ambiguous | Swatches that immediately switch the primary gallery photo and update the visible color label | Single-color products |

## 6. Category default to escape

Cold tech-startup templates, heavy black-and-gold luxury clichés with unreadable
all-caps serif paragraphs, swatches that do not change the product image, and
size charts trapped inside tiny, low-resolution JPEG images that cannot be read
on a phone.

## 7. Optional style directions

| Direction | Fit | Risk | Accessibility cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Warm workshop & tactile craft:** warm paper-toned surfaces, calm editorial headings, macro grain/stitching callouts, and structured specification cards | Leather goods, footwear, bags, heritage batik/tenun, and handmade accessories | Overly dark mood photography can hide pocket/stitching details | Low when product photos are well-lit and text stays on clean surfaces |
| **Editorial lookbook & drape:** generous portrait media proportions (`3:4` or `4:5`), restrained chrome, clear variant swatches, and fluid transitions between outfit and closeup views | Modest fashion, Raya/seasonal collections, sarimbit sets, and contemporary apparel | Large imagery can hurt mobile LCP if not sized and prioritized | Low — ensure sticky mobile purchase bars do not obscure variant errors |
| **Structured catalog & quick-spec:** compact variant availability pills, immediate stock-per-size visibility, and fast multi-size ordering | Reseller/grosir apparel portals, uniform suppliers, and multi-item bundle stores | Can feel transactional if fabric/material storytelling is omitted | Low — keep size-pill touch targets at least 44px |

## 8. Mobile notes

- Keep color swatches, size pills, price, and the size-guide trigger within the
  same mobile decision zone so buyers do not scroll up and down repeatedly to
  check measurements.
- Ensure gallery pinch-to-zoom or tap-to-zoom works smoothly so buyers can
  inspect stitching, grain, and fabric opacity on a phone screen.

## 9. Claim traps

- **Leather grading:** never call synthetic PU/PVC or bonded scrap "Kulit Asli /
  Full-Grain". State the exact material honestly.
- **Handcrafted / origin claims:** only claim handmade stitch construction,
  vegetable tanning, or specific regional heritage (e.g. Garut leather, Pekalongan
  written batik vs printed motif) when verified by the owner.
- **Waterproof vs water-resistant:** distinguish natural water resistance from
  sealed waterproof construction.

## 10. Open questions

1. What are the exact material grades, hardware specifications, and care rules
   for each product line?
2. Do products use single-variant selection, or do any lines require coordinated
   multi-variant/sarimbit set selection?
3. What centimeter measurements (`LD/PB/PL` or `P × L × T` or insole `cm`) are
   available for the size/dimension guide?
4. What is the exact size-exchange or workmanship warranty policy?
