# Food and Beverage

Structure: [README.md](README.md). Covers packaged products and venues
(café, restaurant, catering); say which one the project is. A starting
hypothesis; inspected references and the owner's brand overrule it.

## 1. Job and decision path

- **Packaged product:** entry from social, marketplace, or a reseller →
  taste, ingredients, halal, shelf life, size, price, delivery area → buy or
  become a reseller → reorder.
- **Venue / catering:** entry from maps, search, or social → menu and price,
  location and hours, photos of real dishes → reserve, order delivery, or
  WhatsApp an enquiry (catering: date, headcount, budget).

## 2. Market conventions — VERIFY per project

- Halal status and certificate holder; BPOM (MD/ML) or PIRT registration for
  packaged food in Indonesia; Malaysian equivalents. Confirm current rules and
  the owner's actual numbers.
- Delivery apps (GoFood, GrabFood, ShopeeFood) and marketplaces as the real
  checkout; on-site may only need to route there.
- WhatsApp ordering with a pre-filled message; COD for packaged goods.
- Reseller/agent recruitment pages are common for packaged products.
- Rupiah/RM menu prices; whether tax/service is included.

## 3. Must-have content inventory

- Real photos of the actual product or dish (not stock), menu with prices,
  portion or net weight.
- Ingredients and allergens, halal status, shelf life and storage.
- Location, opening hours, map link, delivery coverage, contact.
- For catering: minimum order, lead time, package options.

## 5. Recurring patterns

| Problem | Pattern | Does not apply when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appetite is visual | Large, honest close-ups of the real product, consistent lighting | no real photos yet (ask; do not generate food that does not exist) |
| "Where and when can I get it?" | Hours, location, and order channels in the first screen on mobile | pure online brand |
| Menu scanning | Real text menu (not an image) with categories and prices | menu changes daily: link to the live source |
| Choosing a channel | One clear primary action plus labelled secondary channels | single channel |
| Catering enquiry quality | Short form or WhatsApp template asking date, headcount, budget | no catering |

## 6. Category default to escape

Warm brown/orange palette, hand-script display font, stock steam-and-wood
photography, "authentic" and "homemade" in every heading, menu delivered as a
PDF or image. Valid when chosen; a default when it came from "food".

## 7. Optional style directions

| Direction | Fit | Risk | A11y cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product-forward packaging colour | snack and drink brands with strong packs | clashes with photography | check text on saturated brand colours |
| Menu-first utility | cafés, restaurants with frequent visitors | feels plain | low |
| Story-led (origin, maker) | heritage or craft producers with real story | delays price and ordering | long pages need clear navigation |

## 8. Mobile notes

Hours, address, and order action without scrolling; tap-to-call and map links;
menu readable as text at 16px; delivery-app buttons labelled by name.

## 9. Claim traps

Health claims ("boosts immunity", "sugar-free" when not), unverified halal or
"no preservatives", "best in town", invented sales numbers or reseller income,
stock photography presented as the actual dish.

## 10. Open questions

Packaged or venue? Registration and halal holder? Which channels take orders?
Delivery coverage? Real photos available? Reseller programme?
