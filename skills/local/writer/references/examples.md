# Worked Patterns

## Humanization

Weak: "In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, businesses must leverage innovative solutions."

Better: "Businesses lose time when orders, payments, and shipping are managed in separate systems."

Reason: replaces generic scene-setting with an operational problem without inventing a result.

## Claim preservation

Source: "The feature can reduce manual entry for some workflows."

Unsafe: "The feature eliminates manual entry."

Safe: "The feature can reduce manual entry in supported workflows."

## Indonesian business copy

Weak: "Kami menghadirkan solusi inovatif yang mampu meningkatkan bisnis Anda secara signifikan."

Better: "Kelola produk, pesanan, pembayaran, dan pengiriman dari satu dashboard."

Use the better version only when those capabilities are verified.

## Technical writing

Weak: "Configure the system appropriately."

Better: "Set `PAYMENT_MODE=sandbox`, restart the service, and confirm that `/health` returns `200`."

Use the better version only when the identifiers and expected result are supplied or verified.
