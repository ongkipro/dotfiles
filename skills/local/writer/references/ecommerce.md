# Ecommerce Writing

## Product pages

Prioritize:

- what the product is;
- who it is for;
- relevant benefits tied to real features;
- specifications, materials, dimensions, compatibility, and care;
- delivery, return, warranty, and limitation details when provided;
- purchase guidance only where shared policies and the user allow it.

Do not invent materials, certifications, origin, compatibility, stock, reviews, delivery time, or performance.

For Shopify, do not add CTA language to descriptions or metadata unless the user explicitly asks. Keep titles, metadata, tags, and ALT text brand-generic unless the user opts in.

## Catalog consistency

Keep naming, units, variant labels, capitalization, and feature order consistent across products. Separate product facts from persuasive interpretation.
