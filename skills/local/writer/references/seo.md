# SEO Writing

## Preserve first

Protect the primary query, search intent, entities, headings, internal-link targets, metadata constraints, and required facts.

## Optimize

- Match the dominant intent: informational, commercial, transactional, navigational, or local.
- Answer the core query early.
- Use headings that describe real sections, not keyword containers.
- Cover necessary subtopics and entities without padding.
- Place keywords naturally; do not target an arbitrary density.
- Make definitions, steps, comparisons, and tables easy to extract when appropriate.
- Use internal links where they help the reader continue a task.
- Write titles and meta descriptions for relevance and click clarity, not hype.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent expertise, reviews, test results, or first-hand experience.
- Do not add "EEAT language" without evidence.
- Do not sacrifice readability to exact-match repetition.
- Preserve canonical product names, locations, and commercial terms.
