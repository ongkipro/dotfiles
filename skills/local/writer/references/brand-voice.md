# Brand Voice

## Build a voice profile

Infer or confirm:

- relationship to audience;
- formality;
- confidence level;
- sentence density and rhythm;
- preferred vocabulary;
- humor and emotional range;
- acceptable urgency;
- words, claims, and tones to avoid.

Use this priority order:

1. Explicit user instructions.
2. User-provided samples or brand guide.
3. Existing terminology in the source.
4. Channel norms.
5. Generic best practice.

Do not confuse voice with ornament. A premium voice can be simple; a technical voice can be readable; a founder voice can be direct without becoming casual.

For imitation, reproduce high-level characteristics, not distinctive phrases from a living author or proprietary source.
