# Quality Scoring and Validation

Score only when requested. Use evidence, not intuition.

## Dimensions (1-10)

- Meaning preservation
- Factual and claim integrity
- Clarity
- Specificity
- Structure and flow
- Naturalness and rhythm
- Audience and brand fit
- Channel fit
- Persuasiveness or task effectiveness
- Format and constraint compliance

## Interpretation

- 9-10: ready to use; only optional refinements remain.
- 7-8: strong, with identifiable improvements needed.
- 5-6: usable but materially uneven or generic.
- 3-4: significant structural, credibility, or goal-fit problems.
- 1-2: misleading, unusable, or fundamentally off-task.

Do not average away a critical failure. A factual invention, broken schema, omitted disclaimer, or changed offer is a separate blocking issue.

## Final report

Provide:

1. overall readiness;
2. blocking issues;
3. top improvements;
4. dimension scores when requested;
5. preservation result: pass, pass with disclosed changes, or fail.
