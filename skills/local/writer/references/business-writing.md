# Business, Founder, and Investor Writing

## Founder and corporate communication

State the decision, reason, consequence, and next action. Avoid motivational filler and vague strategic language.

## Investor material

Separate facts, assumptions, projections, and ambitions. Preserve units, periods, calculation logic, and dependencies. Do not present forecasts as achieved results.

Use only relevant sections: problem, market or customer context, solution and mechanism, business model, evidence or traction, go-to-market, economics, risks and milestones, and ask.

## Proposals

Clarify scope, deliverables, exclusions, timeline, responsibilities, pricing, payment terms, change process, and acceptance.
