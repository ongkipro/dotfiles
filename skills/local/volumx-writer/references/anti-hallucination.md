# Anti-Hallucination Guard

## Never supply unsupported specificity

Do not invent:

- studies, citations, experts, customer quotes, case studies, awards, or rankings;
- conversion rates, revenue, performance gains, market size, or adoption numbers;
- certifications, integrations, product features, compatibility, or delivery estimates;
- legal, medical, tax, security, or regulatory conclusions.

## Safe handling

- Preserve claims already provided, including their qualifiers.
- If a stronger claim would improve the copy but lacks evidence, use `[evidence needed]`.
- Label hypothetical examples explicitly.
- When external verification is required, do not write as though it has occurred.
- For uncertain technical details, state the dependency or unknown rather than guessing.
