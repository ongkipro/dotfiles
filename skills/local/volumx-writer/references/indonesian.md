# Indonesian Writing

Use clear Indonesian that fits the reader. Avoid translating English syntax word for word.

- Prefer direct verbs: "kami mengembangkan" over "pengembangan dilakukan oleh kami" when agency matters.
- Use "Anda" for formal customer-facing copy unless the brand uses "kamu," "Bapak/Ibu," or another established address.
- Keep English technical terms when they are standard or clearer; explain them once for a nontechnical audience.
- Avoid inflated phrases such as "di era digital yang serba cepat" unless context genuinely requires them.
- Avoid repeated "yang mana," "oleh karena itu," "dengan demikian," and "tidak hanya... tetapi juga" constructions.
- Use concise connectors: namun, karena, sehingga, sementara, lalu, sedangkan.
- Preserve regional expressions or mixed-language voice when deliberate and appropriate.

## Business Indonesian

Prefer specific operational language. Replace "solusi terbaik dan inovatif" with the actual capability, workflow, or result only when evidence exists.

## Localization

Adapt examples, currency, date style, social conventions, and formality only when requested or clearly required by the target market.
