# Conversion Writing

## Conversion hierarchy

1. Clarify the audience and desired action.
2. State the relevant problem or desired outcome.
3. Present the mechanism or offer.
4. Support claims with available proof.
5. Resolve material objections.
6. Explain risk, conditions, or next steps.
7. Use one primary CTA hierarchy where the channel and shared policies allow it.

Use AIDA, PAS, BAB, or another framework only when it improves the logic. Do not force every section into a named formula.

## Preserve conversion intent

Protect offer details, price, eligibility, urgency source, CTA, proof, guarantee terms, and exclusions.

## Ethical persuasion

- Never fabricate scarcity, social proof, countdowns, guarantees, or outcomes.
- Do not hide important conditions.
- Use urgency only when a real deadline, capacity limit, or cost of delay exists.
- When a CTA is allowed, make it specific about what happens next.
