# Meaning Preservation

## Protected-content ledger

Before editing constrained text, identify:

- factual claims and evidence;
- numbers, dates, prices, percentages, units, and thresholds;
- names, entities, product labels, and technical terminology;
- uncertainty markers such as may, can, often, estimated, and approximately;
- attribution and citations;
- eligibility, exclusions, dependencies, and conditions;
- disclaimers, legal language, and safety instructions;
- SEO keywords and search intent;
- CTA, offer, audience, and conversion objective;
- code, commands, configuration, schemas, links, and formatting.

## Preservation rules

- Preserve logical force. "Can" is not "will"; "helps" is not "guarantees."
- Preserve scope. "Some users" is not "users."
- Preserve time. Historical evidence must not become a current claim.
- Preserve causality. Do not imply cause when the source states association.
- Preserve ownership and attribution.
- Preserve operational consequences in technical and product writing.
- Preserve required repetition when needed for compliance, scanning, or conversion.

## Disclosure

When an improvement requires changing a protected element, make the change only with explicit user instruction or disclose it clearly after the draft.
