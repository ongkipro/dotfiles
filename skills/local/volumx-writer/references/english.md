# English Writing

- Prefer plain, precise English over corporate inflation.
- Use contractions for conversational voice; avoid them in formal or contractual contexts when appropriate.
- Preserve domain terminology and the brand's spelling convention.
- Avoid repetitive openers, excessive signposting, and generic phrases such as "in today's fast-paced world."
- Use active voice when agency matters; retain passive voice when the actor is unknown, irrelevant, or intentionally de-emphasized.
- Keep parallel structure when it improves scanning; break mechanical symmetry when it becomes repetitive.
